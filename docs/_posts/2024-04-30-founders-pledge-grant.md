---
title: ARAAC researchers awarded $127,000 Founders Pledge grant for AI safety
short: >-
  Professor Peter Vamplew and Associate Professor Cameron Foale have secured
  $127,000 from Founders Pledge to advance multi-objective reinforcement
  learning for safer advanced AI systems.
---

We're thrilled to announce that our own Professor Peter Vamplew and Associate
Professor Cameron Foale have been awarded $127,000 in funding from Founders
Pledge, a UK-based charitable initiative. This grant will support their research
into multi-objective reinforcement learning to enhance the safety of advanced AI
systems.

This project is a collaborative effort with the Australian Responsible
Autonomous Agents Collective, including experts from Federation University,
Deakin University, and the University of New South Wales. Their work focuses on
training AI to balance multiple objectives, ensuring solutions that are not only
effective but also safe and ethical.

As Professor Vamplew puts it, "If you're training a self-driving car, you want it
to be quick, safe, and efficient. Overlooking any one factor can lead to serious
issues. Our goal is to develop AI that can make balanced decisions across all
necessary objectives."

This funding will help employ a research assistant to tackle initial challenges,
laying the foundation for more complex AI safety solutions. We're immensely proud
of this achievement and look forward to seeing the positive impacts of their
research.
