title: ARAAC joins the IASEAI affiliate network
short: >-
  ARAAC has joined the International Association for Safe and Ethical AI (IASEAI)
  as an affiliate, connecting our collective to a global community working
  towards safe and ethical artificial intelligence.
---

We're delighted to announce that the Australian Responsible Autonomous Agents
Collective has joined the [International Association for Safe and Ethical AI
(IASEAI)](https://www.iaseai.org/) as an affiliate organisation. This connects
ARAAC to a worldwide community committed to ensuring that AI systems operate
safely and ethically.

IASEAI is a global, membership-based nonprofit whose mission is to ensure that
AI systems operate safely and ethically, benefiting all of humanity. It connects
experts from academia, policy groups, civil society, and industry to accelerate
research, inform public opinion, and influence policy across four interconnected
areas: community, research, policy, and education. Its global community now spans
2,000 members from 65 countries and more than 130 affiliate organisations.

That mission aligns closely with ARAAC's own work across multi-objective
reinforcement learning, explainability, transparency, and AI safety, drawing on
expertise from Federation University, Deakin University, and the University of
New South Wales. As an affiliate, we look forward to collaborating with the
broader IASEAI network, amplifying our shared impact, and contributing to the
global effort to build AI that is both effective and aligned with human
interests.

We're proud to stand alongside the other organisations in the affiliate network
and excited about the opportunities this connection brings for our researchers
and students.
